## Always-on rules

- Before declaring `ultimate-combined` success, prove HARD GATE #10: 2 physical dirs + 4 real files, 0 symlinks (verify with `find -type f` / `find -type l`).
- TDD RED→GREEN required: record before/after with `ls -la`/`find`, not estimates.
- If told not to stop, complete A→B→gate uninterrupted.
- `ultimate-skills` (router) and `ultimate-skill` (token-opt) are separate layers.
- Quoted paths with spaces require MSYS `/c/`; verify with `ls -la` first.

## Procedure

1. RED: count dirs/files/symlinks.
2. Fix: `mkdir -p` 2 dirs, `cp` 4 files (no `ln -s`).
3. GREEN: `find -type f | wc -l` = 4; `find -type l` = 0.
4. Confirm handoffs; restart if fail.
5. Declare success after green proof.
